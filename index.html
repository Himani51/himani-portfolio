About

I like software that has to be right, not just ship.

I'm Himani Vaghani, an independent engineer who builds at the intersection of AI and security. Most of what I ship exists because something else was quietly wrong — an AI hallucinating an import, a backend missing a rate limiter, an answer that sounded confident but wasn't.

My projects tend to fall into two buckets: tools that audit AI output (DepFirewall, Thought Leak Auditor, WebShield) and full products built with AI in the loop (BrewClaude, JobFlow AI, Prompt Hub). Both need the same instinct — care about the craft, and don't trust the happy path.

  • Ships production code, not just demos
  • Security-first mindset on everything AI-adjacent
  • Comfortable end-to-end: frontend, backend, infra
